Bounded event selection

Every bag has no-fork plus at most 15 unordered pairs drawn from six daughter candidates. The primary margin is fixed at 4.0. Shared external calibration is also used for real-only neural-weight controls. Zoo tracks provide weak geometry supervision; C7 uses explicitly rendered optical patches.
