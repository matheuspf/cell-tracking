The numbers behind “failed criteria”

These are our predeclared engineering criteria, not the Kaggle metric. For each embryo and seed: full R3 and Q3 must improve; float censored mean error must decrease; R7 may drop by at most 0.20 percentage points at full count and at each K=100/200/400. Q3 is the mean of R3 at those three candidate caps.

On 44b6, float censored mean error rises from 1.7524 µm to 1.8343 / 1.7733 µm. At K=400, R7 changes from 544/597 (91.12%) to 541/597 (90.62%) / 542/597 (90.79%): drops of 0.50 / 0.34 points, exceeding the 0.20-point allowance. Seed 20260914 also loses two matches at K=100: 274 → 272.

Both 6bba fits pass that guard. Separately, their close-pair recovery falls from 82/108 to 77/108 and 78/108. This subgroup regression was a diagnostic, not one of the coded pass/fail conditions.

Constant offset

Float error improves on both embryos: 1.7524 → 1.6356 µm on 44b6 and 2.4003 → 2.1408 µm on 6bba. Close-pair recovery improves from 54/75 → 63/75 and 82/108 → 86/108.

It fails on 44b6 solely because K=100 R7 falls from 274/597 (45.90%) to 268/597 (44.89%), a 1.01-point drop. Full-count R7 loses only one match there (586 → 585), within the allowed drop. This distinction matters if the actual tracker uses many more than 100 candidates.

Interpretation: the heads do not yet demonstrate robust localization gains across strata. The constant control is a worthwhile candidate for an end-to-end comparison despite its guard failure. The failures should not be presented as measured final-score regressions.

We should test a larger proposal bank

Existing count sweeps already show a recall benefit from retaining more candidates. K means at most K candidates per frame, selected by the original score; frames with fewer candidates keep all of them. Totals span the same 400 frames.

@@BUDGETS@@

For unchanged Cellpose, K=400 → all retains 52,280 more candidates (+50.30%) and recovers 112 more annotated nodes at 7 µm: 2,173 → 2,285, or 91.19 → 95.89%. This is a detector result, not the score tradeoff. We have not tested a bank larger than this existing “all” bank.

With the current bank and at most 3 µm of correction, an optimistic geometric analysis caps integer recall at 90.14% at 3 µm and 97.52% at 7 µm. At least 59 annotated observations cannot be recovered at 7 µm under these restrictions. Those are conditional bounds: adding proposals or changing the correction range can raise them. They are not limits of Cellpose or of a future detector.

Additional internal candidates have no direct metric cost if final output is unchanged. If emitted, extra nodes reduce the count multiplier; if poorly linked, they can also create evaluable edge/division errors or alter which prediction is matched to a GT node. Useful additional nodes can recover enough correct edges and divisions to improve the combined score. More candidates therefore need selection, not a blanket rejection.

What the official metric rewards

The released scorer was checked against GitHub on 14 September 2026; it matches the installed revision 075fc5f5a52d11077f9dc2b074644618f26939e2. For a clip:

J = TPedge / (TPedge + FPedge + FNedge)
M = 1.1 − 0.1 × Nsubmitted / Nestimated
Jadjusted = max(0, J × M)
Score = weighted mean of Jadjusted + 0.1 × micro-averaged division Jaccard

Clip weights are edge TP+FP+FN. The supplied estimate refers to all true node observations, including unannotated cells; it is not the sparse GT count. Unmatched predictions are not automatically false positives. Edge FP accounting depends on annotated lineage evidence. All submitted nodes still enter Nsubmitted, including isolated nodes. Smaller position errors have no separate score term, but affect one-to-one matching and linking.

Training, validation and the next experiment

The 398,755-parameter head uses frozen CPDINO features from three views, a native 7×25×25 image patch and subvoxel query displacement. The source is always the other embryo. Source GEFF observations at t=9/29/49/69/89 in all source clips provide 1,002 / 5,770 GT observations; center plus jitter queries total 4,008 / 23,080. Supported natural proposals add 595 / 1,673 queries. Unknown real proposals receive no supervised loss. AdamW runs a fixed 3,000 updates at initial learning rate 0.0003 for each direction and seed. These observation sets overlap; they are not independent cells.

All four checkpoints and 824 prediction files were locked before evaluation. The separate constant control was created after examining the head results, using source-only weighted Smooth-L1 fitting; its two offsets and predictions were frozen before its scoring. Integrity checks, geometry checks, cache/raw parity and two fitted raw-volume replays passed. Training isolation is verified at the adaptation stage; inherited CPDINO exposure remains unresolved. Both embryos were previously examined.

Next comparison: evaluate baseline centers, constant correction and the two head variants with the same frozen tracker on complete clips. Cross that with the current bank and two source-chosen broader proposal settings; allow the same tracker to reject candidates. Report combined score, edge TP/FP/FN, division TP/FP/FN, submitted/estimated node ratio, 1–7 µm recall, close-pair recovery and runtime for each embryo. Freeze settings using source data before evaluating the opposite embryo. Treat 1–3 µm localization as diagnosis and the official score as the primary selection criterion. Keep the encoder frozen during this comparison; a denser bank does not require encoder fine-tuning.

Candidate mechanisms include a lower object threshold, splitting merged masks, or a separately trained complementary detector. Denser output must have biological image support; the sparse unannotated region is not known background. Reused-embryo results guide engineering, while an untouched embryo is needed for stronger generalization evidence.
